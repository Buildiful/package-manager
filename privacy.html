Privacy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Package information

Package Manager saves package names, senders, dates, tracking details, notes, statuses and settings in localStorage in your browser or installed web app. Your package records are not uploaded to Buildiful, GitHub, Google Analytics, or any other server by the app. All editing, searching, backup imports/exports and retention cleanup happen on your device.

Storage can be cleared by your browser, device, private-browsing settings, or app removal. Browser tabs and installed Home Screen apps may use separate data stores. The app does not synchronize data across devices. Use the JSON backup export in Settings to keep a separate copy; importing replaces the existing list. Expired completed entries are deleted when the app is running or next opened, not by a background server.

Optional tracking-page links only open when you select them and may send information to the linked carrier's website in the normal course of navigation. Background music is stored with the site and starts only when you turn it on. The app can cache its files for offline use, including the music after it has been played.

Basic website analytics

On the official Buildiful GitHub Pages site, we use Google Analytics 4 to measure page visits and selected basic tool actions so we can improve the tool. We do not intentionally submit package names, tracking numbers, sender details, notes, dates, or search queries as analytics event parameters. The GA4 configuration denies advertising consent and disables Google Signals and ad personalization in the tag; associated Google Analytics property settings must also be checked separately.

When enabled, GA4 may use first-party cookies, including pseudonymous identifiers, and may process technical browser and network information from which an approximate location can be derived. See Google's Privacy Policy and GA4 cookies.

Regional privacy choices

In regions requiring consent for our analytics configuration, you can allow or decline analytics before Google Analytics loads. Where an accessible objection option is required, it is provided here. In other regions where basic analytics can lawfully run without those controls, it starts without a prompt. We honor supported browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals by disabling analytics. If regional classification fails, Google Analytics stays off until permission is given.

Regional check and local preferences

To determine the regional analytics experience, this page may contact IPWhois at https://ipwho.is/. That provider can receive the IP address associated with the request. Only a coarse consent category is saved locally for up to 12 hours. An explicit choice, when made, is saved in browser storage for up to 365 days and can be shared across tools hosted at the same origin. No location coordinates or full IP response are stored by this module.

Revoking consent or objecting stops later measurement from this module. Information previously collected by Google Analytics is not automatically deleted, and previously set cookies may remain until cleared or expired. Google Analytics data retention depends on the property's settings.

Other services and contact

This tool is delivered through GitHub Pages. GitHub and other delivery providers process ordinary network requests independently of Google Analytics. For privacy questions or applicable rights requests, use the contact options on Buildiful's website.
